CG50 Studio

Open your calculator's flash dump

CG50 Studio runs the real fx-CG50 operating system from a copy of your own calculator's flash. No Casio software is included: the OS is Casio's, so you bring a dump of your own calculator.

  1. 1Make a dump of your calculator's flash with a dumper add-in (how).
    The whole 32 MB, storage memory included, is best: your files and add-ins come along, and installing works.
  2. 2Copy the .bin file to this computer.
  3. 3Open it here. CG50 Studio remembers it and resumes right where you left it; change it any time with the dump button at the top.
